⚠️ Service Unavailable

The container for [[.Hostname]] is running but cannot be proxied.

Issue: [[.Warning]]

Service: [[.ServiceName]]

🔧 How to fix

[[if eq .WarningType "no_port"]]

Add an expose directive to your docker-compose.yml:

services:
  [[.ServiceName]]:
    image: your-image
    expose:
      - "8080"  # Replace with your service port

Or use the roji.port label:

services:
  [[.ServiceName]]:
    image: your-image
    labels:
      - "roji.port=8080"  # Replace with your service port
[[else if eq .WarningType "conflict"]]

Multiple services are using the same hostname. Use the roji.host label to assign unique hostnames:

services:
  [[.ServiceName]]:
    image: your-image
    labels:
      - "roji.host=[[.ServiceName]].localhost"  # Use a unique hostname
[[else]]

Check your docker-compose.yml configuration for issues with this service.

[[end]]
